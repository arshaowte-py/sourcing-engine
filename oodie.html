Landed cost≈ ₹960
Less IGST input credit GST-registered importer− ₹130
Effective landed cost≈ ₹830

India domestic lands at ₹650–900 delivered with creditable GST — the two routes converge on cost, so customisation and scale decide, not price alone. A wearable blanket could also be classified as knitted apparel (Ch. 61, higher duty) — we confirm the HS code with a customs broker before the first shipment.

04 · Investment & activation

Three gates — each one optional

Every phase is a go / no-go decision. Nothing is committed beyond the phase in front of us.

PHASE 0 · 2–3 WEEKS

Sampling

₹25–35K
  • 6–10 pcs from 2 India + 2 China suppliers
  • Air courier for China samples
  • Score fabric GSM, stitching, wash test
PHASE 1 · 4–6 WEEKS

Market test

₹1–1.2L
  • 100 pcs India ready stock (~₹700 avg)
  • Shoot, pack, list — Instagram + marketplace
  • Sell at ₹1,499–1,799 · proves demand before big cheques
PHASE 2 · SEASON RUN

Brand run

₹4.5–5L
  • 500 pcs custom China (~$6.5 FOB) — our prints & labels
  • Freight + duties in; part comes back as GST credit
  • ≈ ₹8L revenue potential at ₹1,599 · then extend to robes & weighted blankets

Reaching suppliers

How the first conversations happen
  • IndiaMART — direct phone numbers published; call or WhatsApp the same day.
  • Alibaba RFQ — one posted request brings supplier quotes to us, inbound.
  • Buyer account + Trade Assurance escrow for every China payment.
  • Shortlist rule: verified suppliers, 2+ years, visible transaction history.

Compliance to import

Needed once, before the first China shipment
  • IEC (Import Export Code) — one-time DGFT registration.
  • GST registration — makes IGST at customs claimable as credit.
  • AD code registered with our bank at the port of clearance.
  • Customs broker (CHA) for clearance and HS-code confirmation.
  • None of this is needed for Route B — domestic runs on a normal GST invoice from day one.
05 · Eyes open

Risks, and how we handle them

Seasonality
A warmth product in a warm country.
Handle: peak Oct–Feb (north India + hills), AC-home comfort positioning, Diwali–NY gifting; cooling variants exist for summer.
HS classification
Blanket (Ch. 63, ~32% stack) vs knitted apparel (Ch. 61, higher duty) changes landed cost.
Handle: pre-confirm with CHA; price Phase 2 at the conservative rate.
Quality variance
Factory photos ≠ delivered goods.
Handle: signed golden sample, third-party pre-shipment inspection (~₹10–25K), Trade Assurance escrow.
Competition
Switchover and others already sell at ₹1,595–2,495.
Handle: win on prints and drops (their shelf is mostly solids), sharper entry price, faster reorders via Route B.
