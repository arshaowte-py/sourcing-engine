03 · How the engine works

Same five stages, every batch

S1
Spot
Bestseller lists, review volumes, and trend signals in US / AU / EU D2C.
S2
Validate
Who sells it in India, at what price, with what traction.
S3
Score
Demand · Margin · Ops, each 1–5. Verdict from the scores.
S4
Source
FOB vs domestic wholesale, MOQs, landed cost to Bangalore.
S5
Activate
Named vendors with contacts. See Teardown №1 for the full depth.

What a verdict means

  • Build now — sourcing activated, market test funded from batch budget.
  • Build next — enters sampling as soon as №1's test reads positive.
  • Test — small sample order only; a gate (demand proof or compliance) must clear first.
  • Skip — demand without margin, or margin without a moat. Documented so we don't re-litigate it.
